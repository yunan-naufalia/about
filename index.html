    how I work

    your project

    • ✓

      quote

    • terminology

      key terms checked against official sources (regulations, government and international-organization sites) and kept in a project glossary. your own glossary always comes first.

    • translate & QA

      translated with a translation memory so terms stay consistent, then a separate QA pass for numbers, names, terminology and format.

    • translator's notes

      a review copy with my notes and questions as Word comments, so you can see the key choices and answer queries before the final file.

    • deliver

      a clean file in your original layout, with your feedback applied and one round of revisions included. a translator's statement is available on request.
