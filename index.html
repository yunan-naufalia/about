    Customs Valuation Regulation · ID → EN

    My own translation of Indonesia's Regulation Number 144/PMK.04/2022 on Customs Value for the Calculation of Import Duty, with 38 translator's notes. Here are four excerpts from it.

    Indonesian (source) · Article 2(1)Nilai pabean untuk penghitungan Bea Masuk merupakan nilai transaksi dari barang impor yang bersangkutan yang memenuhi syarat tertentu.
    English (translation)The customs value for the calculation of Import Duty shall be the transaction value of the imported goods provided that certain conditions are met.

    Translator's note “Nilai pabean” is rendered “customs value,” following Article 15.1(a) of the WTO Customs Valuation Agreement (CVA). The term isn’t defined in Article 1 of the regulation and is written in lowercase throughout.

    Indonesian (source) · Article 1, item 1Daerah Pabean adalah wilayah Republik Indonesia yang meliputi wilayah darat, perairan dan ruang udara di atasnya, serta tempat-tempat tertentu di Zona Ekonomi Eksklusif dan landas kontinen yang di dalamnya berlaku ketentuan peraturan perundang-undangan di bidang Kepabeanan.
    English (translation)Customs Territory means the territory of the Republic of Indonesia which includes land, waters and air space above it, as well as certain places in the Exclusive Economic Zone and the Continental Shelf to which the Customs Law applies.

    Translator's note “Daerah Pabean” is rendered “Customs Territory,” following CVA usage.

    Indonesian (source) · Article 1, item 3(d)mereka yang salah satu diantaranya secara langsung atau tidak langsung memiliki, mengendalikan, atau memegang 5% (lima persen) atau lebih saham yang beredar dari salah satu dari mereka;
    English (translation)persons where any person directly or indirectly owns, controls, or holds 5 (five) per cent or more of the outstanding shares of both of them;

    Translator's note The literal wording doesn’t make clear whose shares are held. The English follows Article 15.4(d) of the CVA (“any person … of both of them”) for the ownership test, but keeps the Indonesian text’s “shares” where the CVA says “voting stock or shares.”

    Indonesian (source) · Article 1, item 5Pemilik Barang adalah Importir atau Orang yang meminta Importir mengimpor barang untuk dan atas kepentingannya dan diberitahukan dalam pemberitahuan pabean impor.
    English (translation)Owner of Goods means an Importer or a Person who requests an Importer to import goods on their behalf and in their interest, as declared in the import declaration.

    Translator's note “Pemilik Barang” is a domestic defined term with no CVA equivalent, so it’s rendered descriptively.

    Sustainability Report · ID → EN

    A self-initiated translation of the President Director's message in AIA Indonesia's 2024 sustainability report, with 23 translator's notes. It wasn't commissioned by AIA, and I'm not affiliated with the company. Here are four short extracts.

    Indonesian (source)Nilai pertanggungan mencapai Rp735 triliun
    English (translation)Total sum assured had reached IDR 735 trillion

    Translator's note “Nilai pertanggungan” is “sum assured,” the standard life insurance term. English number conventions apply throughout, so “Rp” becomes IDR.

    Indonesian (source)Rencana Aksi Keuangan Berkelanjutan (RAKB)
    English (translation)Sustainable Finance Action Plan (RAKB)

    Translator's note This is the plan financial services institutions submit to OJK under POJK No. 51/POJK.03/2017. I kept the Indonesian acronym because it names a regulatory filing.

    Indonesian (source)Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Perlindungan Data Pribadi
    English (translation)Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection

    Translator's note This is the standard English title. The source spells it “Perlindungan,” while the statute’s own title uses “Pelindungan.” The English is unaffected.

    Indonesian (source)Direksi berperan aktif dalam merumuskan strategi
    English (translation)The Board of Directors takes an active role in formulating strategy

    Translator's note Indonesian companies have a two-tier board: the Direksi (executive management) is separate from the Dewan Komisaris (the supervisory Board of Commissioners).

    A made-up demo of how I review AI output: check the facts, fix the language, and explain every change. I wrote this task for this page, so it isn't taken from any real project.

    Response reviewTask #0001Demo
    User prompt
    Why does Indonesia have three time zones, and which one is Bali in?
    Great question! I'd be happy to help! Indonesia have three time zones because the country stretches more than 5,000 km from east to west. Western Indonesia Time (WIB) is UTC+7, Central Indonesia Time (WITA) is UTC+8, and Eastern Indonesia Time (WIT) is UTC+10. Bali is in WIB, the same as Jakarta. Would you like to know more?
    Indonesia has three time zones because the country stretches more than 5,000 km from east to west:
    • Western Indonesia Time (WIB): UTC+7
    • Central Indonesia Time (WITA): UTC+8
    • Eastern Indonesia Time (WIT): UTC+9
    Bali is in WITA, one hour ahead of Jakarta, which uses WIB.
    Review
    1. Style Cut the filler opener. It adds nothing and delays the answer.
    2. Grammar The subject is singular: “Indonesia has.”
    3. Accuracy WIT is UTC+9, not UTC+10.
    4. Accuracy Bali uses WITA, so it’s one hour ahead of Jakarta. This was half of the question.
    5. Style Cut the closing question. The answer is complete without it.
